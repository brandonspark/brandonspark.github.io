Bar numbers match the song roadmap. Orange ? = the transcription disagrees with both Songsterr and the chord chart there (bars 96, 100, 108); check by ear.

How to read: thumb-slap the low notes and pop the high ones (G-string notes and octave jumps), as all the transcriptions mark it. Staccato notes are written as an 8th plus a rest; held notes show their real length and tied notes are in brackets. x = dead-note thump. Slides, hammer-ons and vibrato are listed under each section. Brown notes = places where the four transcriptions (Nate Navarro, Dmitry Nesmyslov, Lefty Bassman, Songsterr) disagree, with what was chosen and the time in the record (official video, 0:04 = bar 1).
